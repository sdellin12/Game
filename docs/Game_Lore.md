# The Crucible — Lore Bible

## 1. Researchers & The Shared Catalog ("Infinite Pokédex")
* **Researchers:** Players step into the role of **Researchers** who travel across realities cataloging creatures, relics, and phenomena to build tailored decks for matches.
* **Creative Freedom & Reusable Themes:** Themes are not locked to a single player forever. Every theme, creature lineup, or custom discovery logged by a Researcher is recorded into a shared, ever-expanding archive (an "Infinite Pokédex" of realities). Any player can browse the catalog, pick a theme they like, and shape their Sparks to match it when building a deck.
* **Spark Tuning vs. Unit Transform:**
  * **Shaping / Tuning Sparks:** When building a deck, a Researcher chooses their deck's Color Spark and shapes/tunes their 40 Sparks into the creatures, tools, and abilities they have researched. Deck color is assigned when the deck is created rather than flipping mid-match.
  * **Transform (Unit Mechanic):** Reserved for specific cards or units that physically shift between modes or forms on the board (such as transforming vehicles/mechs or multi-mode creatures).

## 2. The Hook, Mysteries & Future Seasons
* **An Unfolding Mystery:** The true cause behind the shattering of the **Prime Matrix** starts out unknown. Rather than forcing a rigid multi-year timeline upfront, mysteries and new story beats unfold naturally as new cards, creatures, and mechanics are built over time.
* **Family-Friendly Conqueror / Scout Angle:** A dramatic, Saturday-morning-style warlord/conqueror draws realms into clashes to scout which worlds are strong enough to be worth conquering—keeping the tone accessible and fun for both kids and adults while leaving room for future seasons and expansions.

## 3. The Stage: The Relic-Ring
* **Static Arena:** Matches take place in **The Relic-Ring**, a steady, ancient stone-and-metal colosseum suspended between realities. Keeping the arena stable and readable on the TV screen ensures the creatures, Leaders, and action stay front and center without overwhelming younger players.
* **The Ring-Keepers:** Silent, ancient overseers who watch over the Relic-Ring and keep the tournament structure orderly.

## 4. Vanguard Cores, Sparks, Leaders & Elimination
* **40 Sparks per Deck:** Each Leader's **Vanguard Core** sheds **40 glowing Sparks**—matching the 40-card deck. When a creature is played, Sparks weave into the form; when a creature is knocked out, its Sparks unravel visually; its card follows the normal discard rules. This does not refund resources or return cards to the deck. The discovery remains in the shared catalog.
* **Leader Retreats & Resummoning:** A Leader anchors their squad in the Relic-Ring. If knocked out, they pull back outside the ring to recover, requiring a higher cost to resummon back into the fight.
* **Player Elimination:** A Researcher whose health reaches zero or who fails a required draw leaves that match permanently, but can spectate and cheer with reactions. Player revival is not part of the current game. Leader retreat and resummoning remain separate mechanics.

## 5. Ten Color Sparks & Tactical Edge
* **Accessible Baseline + Tactical Edge:** In the initial version, all ten colors share a welcoming, competitive baseline so players aren't locked into rigid archetypes on day one. Each color provides a distinct **tactical edge** and signature flavor while keeping room for varied builds and future expansion:
  * **Red (Drive):** Fast, proactive aggression and early pressure.
  * **Orange (Daring):** High-risk, high-reward swings backed by contingency/recovery lines.
  * **White (Discipline):** Formation defense, coordinated blocks, and disciplined counterstrikes.
  * **Green (Resilience):** Stat growth, enduring boards, and healing/recovery.
  * **Gold (Cooperation):** Protecting allies, lending assistance, and earning bonuses for helpful and straight-up/honest play rather than messy side-deals.
  * **Blue (Insight):** Anticipation, timing windows, information advantage, and answering threats.
  * **Purple (Cunning):** Disrupting opponent plans and milling (moving cards from an opponent's deck to their discard pile).
  * **Black (Ambition):** Paying real costs (life, cards, board pieces, or resources) for heavy power spikes.
  * **Silver (Ingenuity):** Upgrades, modular assembly, and multi-unit combinations.
  * **Teal (Adaptability):** Flexible modes, mode-switching, and pivoting roles on the fly.

## 6. In-Game TV Cutscenes (Future Production)
* Short, lightweight TV intros/cutscenes can play during game setup to introduce the world without slowing down game night:
  1. **The Pull:** Vanguard Cores hum as rifts open, drawing Researchers into **The Crucible** amid an unexplained shattering of the Prime Matrix.
  2. **The Relic-Ring:** Reveals the stone-and-metal colosseum, silent **Ring-Keepers**, and the conqueror scouting realms.
  3. **Sparks & Retreat:** Shows a Vanguard Core shedding **40 Sparks**, defeated creatures unraveling, knocked-out Leaders retreating before escalating resummons, and eliminated Researchers watching from outside the ring. Setup intros are optional future production; combat animations remain brief and nonblocking.
